California city/locality suggestions
===================================

Source: https://github.com/kelvins/US-Cities-Database/blob/main/csv/us_cities.csv
Filtered STATE_CODE=CA, deduplicated and sorted. Includes postal localities as well as incorporated cities; this is not a certified municipal boundary register. Suggestions are editable so new or missing cities can still be entered. City selection does not establish coverage, ZIP codes, or transport pricing.
