# US retirement wave and Market Capacity sheet

Updated 2026-11-22 (Andrew t522u–t525u).

## Data
- Installed capacity comes from the **Market Capacity** sheet of the Our Solution data workbook (admin: download → edit → upload → diff preview → publish → version history). Columns: 市場, 年份, 累積裝置量 GW, 出處, 出處年份, 備註. Markets: Europe, United States, Japan, Taiwan, Australia, China, Other markets, World. Every year is stored; nothing is interpolated at runtime.
- Validation on upload: no negatives; no duplicate market-year; all 8 markets for 2025–2050; Other markets = World − six markets within 0.01 GW; United States every year 2000–2050 and non-decreasing.
- Seed: `data/our-solution-market-capacity.json`, generated by `node scripts/generate-market-capacity.cjs`. Europe, Japan, Taiwan, Australia, China and World reproduce the earlier chart formula exactly (2025→2050 fixed growth; 出處 待補). United States 2000–2025 = IRENA statistics (`data/us-pv-installations.csv`, no AC-to-DC correction); 2026–2050 fixed growth from the 2025 IRENA value to 3,200 GW (the earlier chart's 2050 endpoint; 出處 待補). Other markets = World − six markets.
- Published versions without the sheet use the seed, so the live site needs no new publication.

## Model (lib/us-retirement-wave.ts)
- F(t) = 1 − exp(−(t/T)^α); IRENA/IEA-PVPS 2016 shape factors: early loss 2.4928 (default/main), regular loss 5.3759 (comparison). Lifetimes 25 / 18 (default) / 15 / 12.
- No waste in the installation year: retirement share at age a ≥ 1 is F(a) − F(a−1).
- Replacement: Added(t) = [Cum(t) − Cum(t−1)] + Retired(t); Retired(t) = Σ_{v<t} Added(v) × [F(t−v) − F(t−v−1)]. Replacement panels retire again. Stock before 2000 enters as the 2000 vintage.
- Mass(t) = Σ_{v<t} Added(v) × share × t/MW of vintage v (IRENA 2026: 60/0.73 t before 2014, 60 t in 2024, 52.8 t from 2035, log interpolation).
- Computed server-side on /solutions from the published sheet; the installed-capacity chart reads the same rows.
